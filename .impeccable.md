## Design Context

### Users
Fantasy Premier League managers using Top Scores on iPhone to understand their squad, fixtures, projected points and transfer choices quickly during a gameweek.

### Brand Personality
Premium, confident and football-native. The product should feel like a focused performance dashboard rather than a dense spreadsheet.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark-first native iOS interface with a refined pitch-green foundation. Use purple for expected-points information, green for budget and positive performance, and fixture colours only to communicate difficulty. Favour legible rounded system typography, player imagery and restrained high-contrast surfaces.

### Design Principles
- Make the most decision-relevant squad information immediately scannable.
- Use colour semantically and preserve sufficient contrast without relying on colour alone.
- Keep cards purposeful, compact and consistent with the premium pitch presentation.
- Prefer concise, friendly empty and loading states over stale or misleading data.
- Preserve native iOS accessibility: Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels and reduced visual noise.
