# Top Scores 2.0

English (U.K.)

## Promotional text

Find the next televised match, follow the score on your wrist and delay goal alerts to keep them in time with your stream.

## Description

Top Scores is a completely free app focusing on the UK and top international football.

Along with showing fixtures and scores as you would expect, it also offers some unique features:

* Customise the matches you see and receive notifications for based on the world's most popular competitions and top teams
* Add a custom delay to score notifications to avoid ruining the game if you're watching on TV / streaming
* UK TV listings (if a game is on TV, its kick off notification will also tell you which channel to watch)
* FPL integration - see how you and your mates are doing in real-time

## What’s new

• Updated look and feel

## App Review notes

No account is required to browse football scores. Open Scores and tap a match for details, or open Tables for standings. The options button on Scores includes the TV Guide and match calendar. Live match information depends on the fixture schedule. Fantasy features are optional.
