# Top Scores 2.0

English (U.K.)

## Promotional text

Find the next televised match, follow the score on your wrist and delay goal alerts to keep them in time with your stream.

## Description

Which channel is the match on? Who scored while you were away? Top Scores brings football fixtures, results and UK TV listings together on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Choose the teams and competitions you follow, or browse all matches for a day. Open a fixture for kick-off and broadcast details. As the game unfolds, check the scorers, cards and substitutions. For matches with coverage, explore line-ups, momentum and match statistics.

Use the TV Guide to plan your viewing and the match calendar to look ahead. Check league tables to see what a result means for the title race or the fight to stay up.

Follow a game with Live Activities or glance at your Apple Watch for the score. Watching on a delay? Set a delay for score notifications so a goal alert won’t spoil the moment.

Fantasy Premier League managers can follow their squad alongside the football and see which of their players are involved in a fixture.

## What’s new

• Browse fixtures by day and jump to a date in the match calendar.
• Plan your viewing with the UK TV Guide.
• Open Match Details for line-ups, momentum charts and team statistics where available.
• Follow scores with Live Activities and on Apple Watch.
• See which of your Fantasy Premier League players are involved in a match.

## App Review notes

No account is required to browse football scores. Open Scores and tap a match for details, or open Tables for standings. The options button on Scores includes the TV Guide and match calendar. Live match information depends on the fixture schedule. Fantasy features are optional.
